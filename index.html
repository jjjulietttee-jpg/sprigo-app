Sprigo Get the App

Plant Watering Reminder

Sprigo is a plant watering reminder for iPhone. You tell it what you grow, the pot and the light, and it works out when each plant is thirsty, then sends one calm reminder a day, only when something needs a drink. It has plans for nearly 400 houseplants, a week view with a trip planner, a camera light meter, a photo journal and a home screen widget. No account is needed, your plant list stays on your phone, and there is no plant identification or AI.

What Sprigo does

  • Every plant on its own scheduleThe interval for each plant comes from the species, pot size, pot material, drainage, light and the season, with plans for nearly 400 houseplants, succulents, cacti, orchids, ferns, palms and herbs.
  • It learns how your plants drinkAfter each watering you say whether the soil felt still damp, just dry or bone dry, and Sprigo stretches or shortens that plant's next interval. Skip a plant with one tap when the soil is still wet.
  • One reminder a daySprigo sends a single notification at the hour you choose, only on days when something is due.
  • Week view and trip plannerSee the week ahead. Add your away dates and waterings move to before you leave or the day you return, with risky plants flagged.
  • Camera light meterPoint the back camera at a spot to estimate its light and get a label from low light to direct sun. It is an estimate, not a calibrated instrument.
  • Photo journal, history and widgetKeep photos of each plant in a journal, see the last 8 weeks of waterings, get feeding reminders outside winter, and check who is thirsty from a home screen widget.

Frequently asked questions

What is Sprigo?

A plant watering reminder for iPhone. It tells you when each houseplant needs water based on its pot, soil drainage, light and the season.

Does Sprigo identify plants or diagnose diseases?

No. You choose the plant from a list of nearly 400. Sprigo does not use AI, identify plants from photos or diagnose problems.

Is Sprigo free?

No. Adding plants needs Sprigo Pro: $6.99 a month with a 3-day free trial, or $39.99 a year (US prices).

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and your plant list stays on your phone.

How many notifications will I get?

One a day at the hour you choose, and only on days when at least one plant is due.

What if the soil is still wet when it is time to water?

Skip that plant with one tap. Sprigo asks again after about a quarter of the interval, and you can undo the skip.

Does it work in the southern hemisphere?

Yes. Sprigo guesses your hemisphere from your time zone and shifts the seasons.

Is the light meter accurate?

It estimates lux from the camera's auto exposure. It is good enough to tell low light from a bright window, but it is not a calibrated instrument.

Does it run on iPad or Android?

It is an iPhone app. There is no Android version.
