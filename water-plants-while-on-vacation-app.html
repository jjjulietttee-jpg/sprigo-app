How to Keep Plants Alive While You Are Away

Updated October 2026 · By the Sprigo developer

Short answer. Tell your plant app when you are away so the waterings that fall inside the trip are moved: earlier, to the day before you leave, or later, to the day you come back. In Sprigo you add a trip with start and end dates and the week view shows what moves; if a plant would go too dry, it flags it so you can ask someone to help.

Step by step

  1. Add your tripOpen the week view and add your away dates in Sprigo.
  2. See what movesEvery watering that falls inside the trip is shown with its new day: the day before you leave, or the day you are back.
  3. Look out for risky plantsIf waiting until you return would leave a plant dry far past its interval, Sprigo marks it as risky. Those are the ones to hand to a neighbour.
  4. Water extra on the day before you leaveWater the plants that were moved earlier, then enjoy the trip. The widget on your home screen shows who is thirsty when you get back.

Frequently asked questions

How does Sprigo decide to water before or after the trip?

If the watering is due early enough in the trip, it moves to the day before you leave. Otherwise it moves to the day you return, unless that would leave the plant too dry, in which case it is flagged as risky.

How long can a plant go without water?

It depends on the plant and pot. Cacti and succulents last far longer than ferns or herbs. Sprigo compares the dry stretch to the plant's own interval.

Does the app water my plants for me?

No. It plans around the trip. For longer absences ask someone to water the plants flagged risky.

Can I see the whole week at once?

Yes. The week view shows each day and which plants are due.

Will I get reminders while I am away?

Reminders follow the schedule, so a trip is best added before you leave.

Is the trip planner free?

It is part of Sprigo Pro, which has a 3-day free trial on the monthly plan.

Does it work offline?

Plans and reminders are calculated on your phone with no account. Buying or restoring Pro needs the App Store.
