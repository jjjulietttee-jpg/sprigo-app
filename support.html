Common questions

  • Why did the schedule change? Sprigo adjusts each plant's schedule from your answer after watering ("still damp", "just dry", "bone dry") and from the season.
  • I don't get reminders. Check Settings › Notifications › Sprigo, and the reminder time in Sprigo's settings.
  • Restore my subscription. Open Sprigo › Settings › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • Cancel. Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Sprigo Pro.
