How to Stop Overwatering Houseplants (and Let an App Do the Math)

Updated October 2026 · By the Sprigo developer

Short answer. Houseplants are usually overwatered because one fixed schedule ignores pot, light and season. Give each plant its own interval, check the soil before you water, and stretch the interval when the soil is still damp. Sprigo does this: it sets the interval from pot, soil drainage, light and season, then learns from your answer after every watering.

Step by step

  1. Set the interval from the plant's conditionsA big pot, a dim corner or winter all slow drying. Sprigo weighs pot size, pot material (terracotta dries faster), drainage, light and season when it sets the days.
  2. Check the soil when the reminder comesThe reminder is a nudge to look, not an order. If the soil is still damp, tap skip and Sprigo asks again after about a quarter of the interval.
  3. Tell the app how the soil feltAfter watering, say still damp, just dry or bone dry. Sprigo lengthens or shortens that plant's interval, within limits.
  4. Let the seasons move the scheduleSprigo knows the season for your hemisphere and waters leafy plants about a third less often in winter, and cacti and succulents about half as often.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I water houseplants?

There is no single number. It depends on the plant, pot size and material, drainage, light and season, which is why Sprigo gives each plant its own interval.

Why does a plant in a terracotta pot need water more often?

Terracotta lets moisture evaporate through the walls, so soil dries faster. Sprigo shortens the interval for terracotta pots.

Does a pot without drainage change the schedule?

Yes. Water has nowhere to go, so soil stays wet longer. Sprigo lengthens the interval when a pot has no drainage hole.

What if I always forget to check the soil?

Answer the one-tap soil question when you water. Over a few waterings the interval moves toward what the plant actually does in your home.

Can an app diagnose overwatering?

Not Sprigo. It does not diagnose plant problems or identify diseases. It helps by spacing waterings sensibly and letting you skip when the soil is damp.

Does it work in the southern hemisphere?

Yes. Sprigo guesses your hemisphere from your time zone and flips the seasons accordingly.

Do I get a reminder every day?

Only on days when a plant is due, and then as a single notification.
